# Inglés Profesional · 7/10: emails formales

> Anterior: [Tutoría 6/10](2026-10-06_tutoria-present-simple-continuous-frequency-adverbs.md) · Siguiente: [Clase 14/10: telephoning](2026-10-14_clase-telephoning.md) · Profesora: Cristina Gómez Monsalve

Práctica del apartado 1.6 (emails formales). La profesora **no explica la estructura**; da por visto el vídeo. Lo evaluable es saber **cómo suavizar** una frase.

> [!important] Lo imprescindible
> - En inglés, el **imperativo suena brusco** («*Tell me…*», «*Send me…*»): conviértelo en **pregunta cortés** (*Could you…?*, *Would you…?*, *Can you…?*).
> - **Malas noticias:** introdúcelas con ***I'm afraid…*** o ***I'm sorry…***.
> - **Nada de *I want*:** usa ***I would like to…*** / ***Could we…?***
> - Despedida: ***Regards / Kind regards / Best regards***, no *Bye*.

## Email corregido
**Original (con fallos):** *Hi John, I want meeting tomorrow. Are you free at 10? I need talk about the new project. It's very important, and we have many problems. Tell me if you can come. Bye, Mark.*

Fallos: *I want meeting* (falta *a/to have a*; y es demasiado directo) · *I need **to** talk* (falta *to*) · *Tell me* (imperativo) · *Bye* (cierre pobre) · tono poco profesional · no explica qué problemas.

**Versión mejorada:** *Hi John, **I hope you are well.** **Could we have a meeting tomorrow?** Are you available at 10? I need to talk about the new project, as **I'm afraid we have a few issues**. **Could you let me know if you can come?** Kind regards, Mark.*

Checklist que usa: ¿asunto claro? ¿apertura adecuada? ¿gramática? ¿tono profesional? ¿cierre no abreviado?

## Tabla de «mejora» de frases
| Directo | Más formal |
|---|---|
| I need information about the course. | *I would like to have more information about the course.* / **I'm writing to inquire about the course. Could you give me more information about it?** (*inquire* = pedir información, muy formal) |
| Send me the document. | *Could you (please) send me the document as soon as possible?* |
| I can't come to the meeting. | ***I'm afraid** I can't come to the meeting.* / *I'm not able to attend the meeting. Could we reschedule?* |
| Tell me when you're free. | *Could you let me know when you are **available**?* / *Please inform me about your availability.* |
| We have a problem with the order. | ***I'm afraid** we have a problem with the order.* |

## Ejercicio de escritura: 20 portátiles que no llegan
Pedir (1) qué ha pasado, (2) cuándo llegará, siendo cortés. Ejemplo bueno (suyo):
*Dear Mr X, I hope this email finds you well. I'm writing about our order of 20 laptops. The delivery was supposed to arrive yesterday, but we have not received it yet. Could you please tell me what happened and when we can expect it to arrive? Looking forward to your reply. Kind regards.*

Correcciones que hizo a alumnos: *last week* (sin *the*) · *They were supposed to be here **yesterday*** (sin preposición) · *when is it **supposed to arrive**?* · cierre típico: *Looking forward to your news / Thank you for your help. Kind regards.* · «*Could there have been an issue?*» suena forzado: prefiere *Could you tell me if there has been a problem?*
