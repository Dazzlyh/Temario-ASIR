## Unidad 2: conectores de causa, propósito y resultado
| Función | Conectores | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **Causa** | *because* + oración · *because of / due to* + sustantivo · *since / as* | *We stayed at home **because of** the storm.* · *The company closed early **due to** bad weather.* |
| **Propósito** | *to / in order to* + infinitivo · *so that* + oración | *I'm saving money **in order to** buy a new computer.* |
| **Resultado** | *so* · *therefore* · *as a result* · *consequently* | *The server failed; **as a result**, we lost the data.* |
| **Intensidad + resultado** | *so + adj + that* · *such a + adj + noun + that* | *He was **so tired that** he fell asleep.* · *It was **such a** long meeting **that**…* |
| **Contraste** | *although / even though* + oración | *Although it was late, we kept working.* |

> [!warning] Trampas típicas
> - ***because of* / *due to* + sustantivo**, **no** + oración (✗ *because of it rained*).
> - *so* va **antes** del adjetivo; *such* va antes de *a/an + (adj) + sustantivo*.
> - *in order to* + **verbo base** (✗ *in order for buy*).
> - *although* no lleva *but* en la misma frase.
